# Universal Basic Income and Heckman-Informed Early Childhood Human Capital Investment in India

A hypothesis-driven analysis exploring the potential economic returns to India from combining universal basic income with evidence-based early childhood care and education.

## Overview

This study proposes a hypothesis grounded in existing literature: India's economic outcomes may improve by combining universal basic income with early childhood care and education, informed by the Heckman framework on human capital investment. The analysis examines the theoretical and empirical foundations for this dual-track policy approach.

## Key concepts

- **Universal Basic Income (UBI):** direct income transfers to citizens
- **Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE):** evidence-based interventions targeting developmental outcomes
- **Human capital framework:** drawing on Heckman's work on lifecycle skill formation and the returns to education
